## 💡 **Why This Combo WORKS:**

1. **Contrast:** Rounded (Righteous) vs Geometric (Chillax) creates visual interest
2. **Hierarchy:** Display → Heading → Body guides the eye naturally
3. **Personality:** Bold and funky without sacrificing readability
4. **Performance:** Variable fonts + optimized loading = fast
5. **Trendy:** Following 2025 design trends (bold displays, geometric sans)
